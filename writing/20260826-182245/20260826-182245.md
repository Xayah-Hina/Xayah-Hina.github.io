## What to do, what not to do

### Generative Solver Control

First of all, we need to clarify a most important issue over all specific problems: what on earth do we want to do.

What I really want to achieve is **Inverse Design / Intelligent Simulation**, not **Reconstruction**, which means I'm not meant to go after like "Smoke Reconstruction from sparse view" (PINF series) or "World model". Instead, I'm targeted at making physical solver nowadays in DCC intelligent.

In other word, my research objective is lying on synthetic world, not real world. My final task is to help artists' production painless.

$$
\boxed{ \text{Artistic Intent} \rightarrow \text{Physical Controls} \rightarrow \text{Simulation} }
$$

We may call this as:

$$
\boxed{\text{Generative Solver Control}}
$$

### AI for Control, not AI for generation
