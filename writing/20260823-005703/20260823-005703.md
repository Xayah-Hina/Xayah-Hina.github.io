## Keyframe Control of Smoke Simulations【1】

I'm writing to review the (maybe the most) classic differentiable simulation paper.

## Forward Problem

$$
q_0 \xrightarrow{S} q_1 \xrightarrow{S}q_2 \xrightarrow{S}\cdots\xrightarrow{S}q_T
$$

### Initial State $q_0$

Forward simulation problem had been done well then (2003).

Given initial states including

* initial density $S_\rho$,
* initial velocity $V$,
* temperature $S_T$,
* buoyancy $B$,
* viscosity $\mu$,
* boundary condition,
* external force $V_F$,

a forward simulator tells the succeeding motion of the smoke.

However, in actual animation production, there is a big problem:

> A forward simulator is good at telling you what will happen, but you CANN'T tell it what you want.
