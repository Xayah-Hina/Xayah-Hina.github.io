Xayah Tasks

Privacy Policy

A focused calendar connection with deliberately limited access.

Effective September 2, 2026

What the integration accesses

Xayah Tasks requests only Google Calendar's calendar.app.created permission. It creates and manages a dedicated secondary calendar named “Xayah Tasks.” It cannot read or change your existing calendars.

Data used for synchronization

Task codes, titles, project context, session plans, outcomes, completion state, and scheduled times may be written to the dedicated calendar. Changes to those managed events are read back so the task timeline stays synchronized.

Storage and security

Task data and synchronization metadata are stored by the site's Cloudflare backend. The Google refresh token is encrypted at rest with AES-GCM and is used only by the server to synchronize the dedicated calendar.

Sharing and advertising

Google Calendar data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared for unrelated purposes. It is processed only by Google and the infrastructure providers required to operate this personal site.

Disconnecting and deletion

Disconnecting Google Calendar from the Tasks page deletes the dedicated calendar and removes the stored connection credentials. You may also revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions.
